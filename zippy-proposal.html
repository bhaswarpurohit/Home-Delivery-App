5 Core Operational Safeguards & Workflow Engines

5.1 Authoritative Booking Finite State Machine (FSM)

All booking mutations are governed strictly by the NestJS backend; client applications cannot mutate statuses directly. Every state transition is cryptographically audited through server commands to prevent skipped steps, fraudulent billing, or double payouts:

Stage 1
REQUESTED & SEARCHING
Customer completes 3-tap checkout. Payment verified via Razorpay webhook. BullMQ checks favorites, then triggers 800m PostGIS query.
Stage 2
OFFERED_HELPER
Locked to top candidate for 45s. Helper app rings audible alert modal. Auto-cascades to Candidate 2 upon timeout.
Stage 3
IN_TRANSIT & ARRIVED
Helper marks transit. Masked call active. Upon arrival, server verifies device GPS coordinates are within 200m of customer address.
Stage 4
IN_PROGRESS & REVIEWED
Start-Job OTP validated in Redis. End-Job OTP validated to calculate duration, credit partner ledger, and open customer rating screen.

5.2 Tiered Geospatial Dispatch Waterfall with Favorite Helper Priority

Achieving a 10-minute arrival SLA requires searching tight, localized nano-markets rather than broad citywide radiuses:

  • Step 0: Favorite Helper Priority (30s Window): If customer has marked a previous helper as favorite and they are online within corridor, system gives them an exclusive 30s priority booking offer.
  • Step 1: Primary Nano-Market Corridor (r₁ ≤ 800m): PostGIS queries for partners toggled ONLINE, verified for skill category, within 800m. Ranks top 10 candidates by proximity, rating, and completion rate.
  • Step 2: 45-Second Audible Lockout: BullMQ locks booking to Candidate 1 and fires high-priority FCM notification with loud audio ringtone. Helper App presents 45-second modal displaying task scope, distance, and earnings.
  • Step 3: Secondary Radial Expansion (r₂ ≤ 1,500m): If Candidate 1 declines or times out, offer cascades immediately to Candidate 2. If primary pool is exhausted, radius widens to 1,500m, adjusting customer ETA to 15 minutes.
  • Step 4: Operations Board Escalation: If no partner accepts in secondary pool, booking transitions to UNASSIGNED, alerting Admin Operations Console for manual override.

5.3 Cryptographic Dual-OTP & 200m Geofence Verification Protocol

To eliminate "buddy punching," phantom arrivals, and disputed duration claims, task execution enforces dual physical and cryptographic gates:

Verification Lifecycle

• Arrival Check: Helper taps ARRIVED. Server validates device GPS coordinates are within 200m of the residence.

• Start-Job OTP: Customer App generates a 4-digit code (10-min TTL). Customer shares verbally at door; helper enters it, verified against Redis before moving to IN_PROGRESS.

• End-Job OTP: Matching code validated upon completion to record authoritative timestamp and credit earnings ledger.

Anti-Fraud Safeguards

• Geofence Violation Audit: Submitting an OTP beyond 200m generates an immutable PROXIMITY_VIOLATION audit flag, pausing automated payout disbursement until manager review.

• Brute-Force Lockout: 3 consecutive failed OTP entries triggers an automatic 5-minute system cooldown lockout.

• Delay Compensation Trigger: If arrival exceeds promised ETA by >10 mins, system auto-credits ₹50 to customer account.

Worker Safety Rail ("Kavach") & Call Masking: The Helper Partner App features a persistent emergency SOS button on active jobs. Tapping it captures live GPS coordinates and fires high-priority audible alerts across all active Admin Operations screens. All voice communications between customer and partner are anonymized via Twilio/Exotel virtual telephony relay, ensuring zero leakage of personal phone numbers.
